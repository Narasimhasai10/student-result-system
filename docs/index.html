About the System

Built like a registrar's ledger, run like modern software.

Aditya university replaces spreadsheets and paper marksheets with a single source of truth. Faculty enter marks once; grades, percentages and pass/fail status are calculated automatically and made available to students the moment a result is published.

  • 01 Faculty add students and record internal + external marks per subject.
  • 02 The system computes totals, grades, percentage and CGPA automatically.
  • 03 Results move from Draft to Published with a single action.
  • 04 Students sign in to view marks, download and print their marksheet.
